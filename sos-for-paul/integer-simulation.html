The model, numerical method & reading notes

The simulation follows Definitions 1.1 and 1.4 of the paper. Heights are integers on I = {−N, …, N}; all exterior heights are zero.

P(φ) ∝ exp[−β H(φ)]
H(φ) = 2 ∑i<j; i,j∈I |φ(i) − φ(j)|q / |i − j|α + 2 ∑i∈I bi |φ(i)|q
bi = ∑r=N+i+1∞ r−α + ∑r=N−i+1∞ r−α

The factor 2 implements the ordered-pair sum as printed in the definitions. Exterior tails use Euler–Maclaurin summation. Symmetric single-site and interval shifts are accepted with probability min(1, exp[−β ΔH]). There is no imposed height cutoff.

The variance estimate uses the model’s exact sign symmetry: E[φ(0)] = 0. Error estimates use batches of 64 saved squared heights and can underestimate slow correlations. Warmup is a setting, not a convergence guarantee; cold chains and small q can mix slowly. Zero observed variance does not establish localisation.

Size-experiment error bars are approximate standard errors, taking the larger of the variation between seeds and the combined batch estimates. For q = 2, the dashed curve is the exact finite-volume real-valued Gaussian reference, computed from (4βL)−1; the sampled model remains integer-valued.
