The Kozma–Nitzan conjectures and \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) for site percolation

Anthropic's Lean proof that \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) for Bernoulli bond percolation on \(\mathbb{Z}^d\), \(d \ge 2\), which is Kozma and Nitzan's Conjecture 3, was produced by Claude under the direction of Justin Leder. Building on it, ChatGPT 5.6, which I prompted and directed, proved that \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) also holds for Bernoulli site percolation on \(\mathbb{Z}^d\), \(d \ge 3\), and proved Kozma and Nitzan's Conjectures 1, 2, 4, and 6 and Questions 5, 7, and 9, each in a form at least as strong as stated.

All of these proofs are checked by Lean, using Mathlib alone. The formalizations were written by Claude Fable 5.1 (the Kozma–Nitzan results) and by ChatGPT 5.6 with Claude Opus 5 and GLM 5.3 (site percolation).
