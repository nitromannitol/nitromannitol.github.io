\(\theta(p_c) = 0\) and the Kozma–Nitzan conjectures

Statement

For Bernoulli bond percolation on \(\mathbb{Z}^d\), \(d \ge 2\), the probability \(\theta(p)\) that the origin lies in an infinite open cluster satisfies \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) at the critical probability, in every dimension. This is Kozma and Nitzan's Conjecture 3. The same conclusion, \(\theta(p_c) = 0\), also holds for Bernoulli site percolation on \(\mathbb{Z}^d\), \(d \ge 3\). Kozma and Nitzan's four further conjectures, Conjectures 1, 2, 4, and 6, and three of their four further questions, Questions 5, 7, and 9, are also proved, each in a form at least as strong as stated. Question 8 fails under the reading where every minimizer must satisfy it, a distinction that matters only when minimizers tie.

What the AI did

Anthropic's Claude proved \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) for bond percolation, under the direction of Justin Leder, and formalized the proof in Lean. On top of that proof, ChatGPT Sol 5.6 Ultra produced complete proofs of Kozma and Nitzan's remaining conjectures and questions in about ten hours, and Claude Fable 5.1 formalized these in Lean. ChatGPT 5.6 then extended the argument to hypergraphs, giving \(\theta(p_c) = 0\) for site percolation as well, and formalized the extension in about 100,000 lines of Lean with support from Opus 5 and GLM 5.3.

What I did

I directed the hypergraph extension and reviewed the resulting proofs and their Lean formalizations.
