Kozma–Nitzan Conjecture 1 as a corollary of the Lean proof of θ(pc) = 0

The Lean 4 / Mathlib development θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd in all dimensions d ≥ 2 (written by Claude, Anthropic, under the direction of Justin Leder; Lean v4.32.0, Mathlib commit 81a5d257) states in its README, proof guide and metadata that Kozma–Nitzan's Conjecture 1 is not claimed beyond three relays ("Not claimed: Kozma–Nitzan's Conjecture 1 beyond three relays"). Its own theorems already imply the full conjecture.

Conjecture 1 (Kozma–Nitzan, arXiv:2401.12397, p. 3). For Bernoulli bond percolation on any finite graph with arbitrary edge probabilities, any nonempty set A of vertices and any vertices o, b,

P(o ↔ b) ≥ P(o ↔ A) · mina ∈ A P(a ↔ b).

The three Lean files below prove this for every finite weighted graph, every nonempty finite A and all o, b, from theorems already in the development, and Lean accepts the proof with exactly the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound and no sorry.

Files

KN1Statement.lean The statement from Mathlib alone (KN1Statement.MultiplicativeGluing); its three definitions are definitionally equal copies of the development's.
KozmaNitzanConjecture1.lean The proof from the development's theorems: the theorem KN1Corollary.kozmaNitzan_conjecture1, a short file (its declarations live in a fresh namespace, so they cannot be mistaken for theorems of the development).
KN1Bridge.lean The Mathlib-only statement proved, by definitional unfolding, from the theorem of KozmaNitzanConjecture1.lean.
verification.log Recorded clean-build summary, the three compilations with #print axioms, and the development's own axiom-audit output.
SOURCES.sha256 SHA-256 of the development's 251 Lean source files and three pin files (254 entries) that were checked.
LICENSE, NOTICE Apache License 2.0, and the development's NOTICE reproduced as that licence requires.
README.md The exact commands to re-run the check, and attribution.

How to check

shasum -a 256 -c /path/to/SOURCES.sha256  # from the development's root: same sources as checked here
lake exe cache get && lake build          # build the development, as in its README
lake env lean -o .lake/build/lib/lean/KozmaNitzanConjecture1.olean KozmaNitzanConjecture1.lean
lake env lean -o .lake/build/lib/lean/KN1Statement.olean KN1Statement.lean
lake env lean KN1Bridge.lean

Expected: the #print axioms lines of verification.log, each [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], no errors, no sorry.
