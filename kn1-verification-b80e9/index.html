The conjectures and questions of Kozma and Nitzan

Kozma and Nitzan (arXiv:2401.12397) reduced θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd, d ≥ 2, to an inequality on finite graphs, their Conjecture 3, and posed four further conjectures and four questions on the correlations of clusters in finite graphs: Conjectures 1, 2, 4 and 6 and Questions 5, 7, 8 and 9. The Lean 4 / Mathlib development θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd in all dimensions d ≥ 2 (Claude, Anthropic, under the direction of Justin Leder) proves Conjecture 3. It does not claim Conjecture 1, but Conjecture 1 is a relatively easy consequence of an inequality it proves on the way: ordering the vertices x of A by P(x ↔ b), the probability that o reaches x before any earlier vertex of A, times P(x ↔ b), summed over A, is at most P(o ↔ b); pulling out the minimum gives P(o ↔ A) · minx∈A P(x ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b), which is Conjecture 1 for every finite weighted graph.

Here the remaining conjectures and questions of the paper are proved on top of that development: Conjectures 2, 4 and 6 and Questions 5, 7 and 9, each in a stronger form than stated, and Conjecture 1 as above. Question 8, as printed, has a negative answer when its minimiser is not unique, and is open otherwise. The proofs are in the summary (PDF); the Lean files are in lean/.

The results

Bernoulli bond percolation on a finite weighted graph (independent pairs with weights in [0,1], an absent edge being weight 0), a nonempty set A of vertices, vertices o and b, Cx the open cluster of x, and an increasing real function F of vertex sets:

  • Conjecture 4 (p. 32), with the minimiser specified: if a ∈ A minimises E F(Cx) over A, then E[F(Ca); o ↔ A] ≤ E[F(Co); o ↔ A]; hence minx∈A E[F(Cx); o ↔ A] ≤ E[F(Co); o ↔ A], also in the paper's vocabulary of monotone cluster properties f(v, ω).
  • Conjecture 2 (p. 3), in the pre-FKG form (3): minx∈A P(x ↔ b, o ↔ A) ≤ P(o ↔ b, o ↔ A); hence minx∈A P(o ↔ A, x ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b).
  • Question 7 (p. 36), answered affirmatively: if a ∈ A minimises P(a ↔ b) over A, then P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b, o ↔ A), the paper's display (41).
  • Conjecture 1 (p. 3): P(o ↔ A) · minx∈A P(x ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b); and Conjecture 3 (p. 15) from it.
  • Question 5 (p. 32), answered affirmatively: there are coefficients ca ≥ 0, a ∈ A, summing to 1 and not depending on b, with Σa∈A ca P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b) for every b, the paper's display (38).
  • Conjecture 6 (p. 34), without either of its endpoint hypotheses: for e = {v, w} and a ∈ A minimising PG(x ↔ b) over A, PG⏚e(v ↔ b, v ↔ A) ≥ PG⏚e(a ↔ b, v ↔ A), where G⏚e is the graph with the edge e forced open; the paper's display (40) follows, and its hypothesis (39) is itself a consequence of Conjecture 1.
  • Question 9 (p. 36), answered affirmatively: if a ∈ A minimises PH(a ↔ b) over A, where H is the graph with all edges at o removed, then (41) holds in G.
  • Question 8 (p. 36) takes the minimiser of P(a ↔ b, o ↮ A) over A. When P(o ↮ A) = 0 every a ∈ A minimises, and (41) can fail: on the path o – a1 – a2 – b with weights 1, 1/2, 1 and A = {a1, a2}, P(o ↔ A, a2 ↔ b) = 1 while P(o ↔ b, o ↔ A) = 1/2. With a unique minimiser the question is open; it is equivalent to the every-minimiser form restricted to P(o ↮ A) > 0, and it reduces to weights in (0, 1).

In print, Conjectures 2 and 4 are proved for two relays (Theorems 1 and 7), for three relays under extra hypotheses (Theorems 2, 3 and 11), for an observer isolated in G ∖ A and its one-vertex extension (Theorems 4 and 5), and, for every graph and every relay set, for the cluster properties f = 1{|C| ≥ k} with k ≤ 4 (Theorem 9).

The method

The development's inequality above is proved with the clusters of the vertices of A conditioned on avoiding a given set Y of vertices; the vertices of A are ordered by E[F(Cx) | x ↮ Y], and Σx P(o ↮ Y, x is the first vertex of A reached by Co) · E[F(Cx) | x ↮ Y] ≤ E[F(Co); o ↮ Y, o ↔ A]. With Y = {a} for the vertex a of least mean and the function φa(K) = F(K) − E F(Ca in the graph with all pairs meeting K removed), which is increasing in K, every conditional mean on the left is nonnegative, and this is Conjecture 4 with the minimiser specified. Conjecture 6 and Question 9 come from the same inequality with the union of the clusters of several vertices in place of Co: the union of the clusters of v and w does not depend on the state of the edge {v, w}, and the cluster of o is the union of the clusters of o and its open neighbours in the graph with the edges at o removed.

Implications

  • The paper's chain 4 ⟹ (3) ⟹ 2 ⟹ 1 ⟹ 3 ⟹ θ(pc) = 0 holds with every link proved, the last in the development.
  • The vertex of A minimising P(x ↔ b) is itself a valid choice in the pre-FKG comparison (Question 7), uniformly in the graph and in the number of vertices of A; the same holds for the minimiser in the graph with the edges at o removed (Question 9).
  • Conjecture 6 holds without its endpoint hypotheses, so the paper's Lemma 13 applies without them.
  • The conditioned inequality of the method needs no nonnegativity of F and holds for the union of the clusters of any finite set of vertices; it is available for further use.
