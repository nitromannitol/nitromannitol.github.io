Kozma–Nitzan Conjecture 1 as a corollary of the Lean proof of θ(pc) = 0

Unlisted verification page, 2 September 2026. Produced entirely by Claude Fable 5.1 (max) and ChatGPT 5.6 sol (ultra).

The Lean 4 / Mathlib development θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd in all dimensions d ≥ 2 (written by Claude, Anthropic, under the direction of Justin Leder; Lean v4.32.0, Mathlib commit 81a5d257) states in its README, proof guide and metadata that Kozma–Nitzan's Conjecture 1 is not claimed beyond three relays ("Not claimed: Kozma–Nitzan's Conjecture 1 beyond three relays"). Its own theorems already imply the full conjecture.

Conjecture 1 (Kozma–Nitzan, arXiv:2401.12397, p. 3). For Bernoulli bond percolation on any finite graph with arbitrary edge probabilities, any nonempty set A of vertices and any vertices o, b,

P(o ↔ b) ≥ P(o ↔ A) · mina ∈ A P(a ↔ b).

The three Lean files below prove this for every finite weighted graph, every nonempty finite A and all o, b, from theorems already in the development, and Lean accepts the proof with exactly the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound and no sorry.

How the proof goes

For an increasing F ≥ 0 of vertex sets, order the relays by E F(Ca) and let Poa be the event that a is the first relay of o. The development proves, for every finite relay set and all weights in [0,1],

Σa ∈ A P(Poa) · E F(Ca) ≤ E[F(Co); o ↔ A]

(AGloc.gen_firstRank_of_surplusTransfer, fed by the conditioned slack hierarchy CSH.cshAll and the closure over weights 0 and 1 in CSH.surplusTransfer_of_nondegenerate). With F = 1{b ∈ ·} the left side is Σa P(Poa) P(a ↔ b) and the right side is at most P(o ↔ b). The first-relay events partition {o ↔ A}, so their probabilities sum to P(o ↔ A), and pulling out the minimum gives the conjecture. The development's additive gluing theorem comes from the same inequality and then discards the factor P(o ↔ A) by bounding it by one; the file here keeps it. No new mathematical idea is involved.

Status

  • Correctness rests on the development's kernel-checked chain; the development has not been externally refereed, and the corollary inherits that status.
  • The development's stated GitHub repository was not publicly accessible on 2 September 2026, so it is not redistributed here; its Lean sources and build pins are pinned by hash.
  • Not run through the Palomar comparator, which compares only the development's two θ(pc) = 0 statements.
  • Nothing is claimed about Kozma–Nitzan's Conjectures 2 and 4, which are stronger: Conjecture 2 is the pre-FKG form P(o ↔ b) ≥ mina P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b), and Conjecture 4 restricts both expectations to {o ↔ A}; the first-relay bound reaches neither.
  • The implication was anticipated inside the development: a module docstring in Percolation/Continuity/CSH/Defs.lean records that the first-relay bound gives "the additive and multiplicative gluing inequalities". What the release does not contain is a theorem stating the multiplicative form for a general relay set (only the three-relay case, CovTau.kn_conj1_three), and its README, proof guide and metadata disclaim it; the files here supply that theorem.

The mathematics is entirely the development's; an internal docstring of its file Percolation/Continuity/CSH/Defs.lean already notes that the first-relay bound yields "the additive and multiplicative gluing inequalities", in spite of the release-level disclaimers, and its proof guide displays the intermediate estimate P(o ↔ A, o ↮ b) ≤ t·P(o ↔ A) before weakening it.
