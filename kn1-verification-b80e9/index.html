Kozma–Nitzan's conjectures and questions: what is machine-checked and what is not

Kozma and Nitzan (arXiv:2401.12397) reduced θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd, d ≥ 2, to an inequality on finite graphs, their Conjecture 3, and left eight further numbered items open: Conjectures 1, 2, 4 and 6 and Questions 5, 7, 8 and 9. The Lean 4 / Mathlib development θ(pc) = 0 for Bernoulli bond percolation on ℤd in all dimensions d ≥ 2 (Claude, Anthropic, under the direction of Justin Leder; Lean v4.32.0, Mathlib commit 81a5d257) proves Conjecture 3. This page records the status of the other eight on top of that development. Six of the nine items are machine-checked: Conjectures 1, 2, 3 and 4 and Questions 5 and 7, by the Lean files in lean/, each theorem accepted with exactly the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound and no sorry (VERIFICATION.log). Conjecture 6 and Question 9 are proved in the accompanying document (PDF, TeX) and not yet machine-checked. Question 8 is open.

Machine-checked

Bernoulli bond percolation on a finite weighted graph (vertices Fin n, independent pairs with weights in [0,1], an absent edge being weight 0), a nonempty set A of vertices, vertices o and b, and an increasing real function F of vertex sets:

  • Conjecture 4 (p. 32), with the minimiser specified: if a ∈ A minimises E F(Cx) over A, then E[F(Ca); o ↔ A] ≤ E[F(Co); o ↔ A]; hence minx∈A E[F(Cx); o ↔ A] ≤ E[F(Co); o ↔ A] (conjecture4Fixed_holds, conjecture4_holds); also in the paper's vocabulary of monotone cluster properties f(v, ω) (conjecture4_clusterProperty_holds).
  • Conjecture 2 (p. 3), in the pre-FKG form (3): minx∈A P(x ↔ b, o ↔ A) ≤ P(o ↔ b, o ↔ A); hence minx∈A P(o ↔ A, x ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b) (conjecture2Strong_holds, conjecture2_holds).
  • Question 7 (p. 36), answered affirmatively: if a ∈ A minimises P(a ↔ b) over A, then P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b, o ↔ A), the paper's display (41) (question7_holds).
  • Conjecture 1 (p. 3): P(o ↔ A) · minx∈A P(x ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b) (conjecture1_holds, from (3) by the Harris inequality).
  • Conjecture 3 (p. 15), from Conjecture 1 (conjecture3_holds).
  • Question 5 (p. 32), answered affirmatively: there are coefficients ca ≥ 0, a ∈ A, summing to 1 and not depending on b, with Σa∈A ca P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b) ≤ P(o ↔ b) for every b, the paper's display (38) (question5_holds; from Conjecture 4 for the functions F(K) = Σb yb 1{b ∈ K} with y ≥ 0, question5_dual, and finite-dimensional separation).
  • Conjecture 6 (p. 34), reductions: the inequality PG⏚e(v ↔ b, v ↔ A) ≥ PG⏚e(a ↔ b, v ↔ A) implies the paper's display (40) (conjecture6_of_conjecture6Strong); the paper's hypothesis (39) is a consequence of Conjecture 1 (conjecture6_hypotheses_vacuous); that inequality holds whenever a minimises PG⏚e(x ↔ b) over A (conjecture6Strong_of_forceOpen_min); and, for S = {v, w} and any event H determined by the union of the clusters of v and w, E[(F(Cv ∪ Cw) − F(Ca)); S ↮ a, H] = E[φa(Cv ∪ Cw); S ↮ a, H] with φa(K) = F(K) − E F(Ca in the graph with all pairs meeting K removed), together with E[(F(Cv ∪ Cw) − F(Ca)); S ↮ a] ≥ 0 when E F(Ca) ≤ E F(Cv) (PairSource). Conjecture 6 itself is not machine-checked.

In print, Conjectures 2 and 4 are proved for two relays (Theorems 1 and 7), for three relays under extra hypotheses (Theorems 2, 3 and 11), for an observer isolated in G ∖ A and its one-vertex extension (Theorems 4 and 5), and, for every graph and every relay set, for the cluster properties f = 1{|C| ≥ k} with k ≤ 4 (Theorem 9). Both were open in general.

Proved in the document, not machine-checked

  • Conjecture 6, in the form PG⏚e(v ↔ b, v ↔ A) ≥ PG⏚e(a ↔ b, v ↔ A) for e = {v, w} and a ∈ A minimising PG(x ↔ b) over A, where G⏚e is the graph with the edge e forced open; display (40) follows, without the paper's hypothesis (39). The proof conditions on the union of the clusters of v and w, which does not depend on the state of e, and runs the development's argument for that union in place of a single cluster. A Lean formalization is in progress.
  • Question 9 (p. 36): the minimiser of PH(a ↔ b), H the graph with all edges at o removed, satisfies (41). The proof conditions on the edges at o and applies the same argument to the set of open neighbours of o, a set of arbitrary size. Not machine-checked.

Not settled

Question 8 (p. 36) takes the minimiser of P(a ↔ b, o ↮ A) over A, with o not joined to A. The document's Question 8 argument instead uses the minimiser of P(o ↔ A, a ↔ b), for which (41) is the machine-checked form (3) of Conjecture 2; that is a different question. For the paper's Question 8, in the reading where (41) must hold for every minimiser, there is an exact four-vertex counterexample when P(o ↮ A) = 0, where the minimand vanishes identically. In the reading with a unique minimiser no counterexample is known, and no proof is known.

Implications

  • The paper's chain 4 ⟹ (3) ⟹ 2 ⟹ 1 ⟹ 3 ⟹ θ(pc) = 0 is formal from top to bottom: the first four implications in Conjectures.lean, the last in the development (its formalization of the paper's Theorem 6).
  • Of the paper's nine numbered items, six are machine-checked (Conjectures 1–4, Questions 5 and 7), two are proved in the document but not yet in Lean (Conjecture 6, Question 9), and one remains open (Question 8).
  • The vertex of A minimising the unconditional connection probability P(x ↔ b) is itself a valid choice in the pre-FKG comparison (Question 7), uniformly in the graph and in the number of vertices of A.
  • Conjecture 6 holds without either of its endpoint hypotheses (document); hypothesis (39) is in any case a consequence of Conjecture 1 (Lean).
  • The inequality of genY_all, which conditions the cluster of each x ∈ A on avoiding a given set Y and needs no nonnegativity of F, is available for further use; run for the union of the clusters of a set of vertices of every size it would settle Question 9 by the document's argument, and for two vertices it gives Conjecture 6.
