Lean formalizations

I am auto-formalizing all of my papers in Lean. This is an experimental science, and the strategy changes with each new model. All of the code in the repositories below was written by AI agents: Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi and Mistral agents. A majority was written by Anthropic agents, followed by OpenAI agents. What works best at the moment (November 2026) is a single Opus 5.5 xhigh agent directing worker agents, such as Terra or Haiku agents. All of the formalizations are built on Mathlib, and I am collecting the shared dependencies between my projects in the library Lattice-Probability.
